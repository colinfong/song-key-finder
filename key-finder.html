    How it decidesIt averages the song's note content into 12 values and correlates them with the Krumhansl–Schmuckler profiles for every major and minor key.

    Common mix-upsA major key and its relative minor use the same notes, so they often score close together. If the song feels sad, check the minor option.

    LimitsSongs that change key, are mostly drums, or are tuned far from A = 440 Hz give weaker results. The chord chart is an estimate: check it by ear before relying on it.
